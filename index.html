OUR SHARED PURPOSE

Care without boundaries.
Compassion for every creature.

Get to know us ↗

Based in Jamshedpur, Jharkhand, Jan Seva Sangh Trust brings animal care and community action together. Its work includes rescue, medical support, shelter and rehabilitation, alongside awareness that encourages kindness to animals.

RESCUE, TREATMENT & CARE

Support through every stage.

Explore all projects ↗
01 / RESCUE

Help for animals in distress

The Trust’s rescue work includes field support and initial care for injured or sick animals. Contact the team to ask what help is available.

02 / RECOVERY

Treatment and recovery

Published programmes include medical care, surgery and rehabilitation. Recovery support helps animals beyond the first treatment.

03 / SHELTER

Shelter and ongoing care

The Trust describes shelter and longer-term care for rescued animals, including animals that are old or living with disabilities.

Cows and cattle

Cattle rescue and treatment are part of the Trust’s published work. Read the accounts of care in Sakchi, Telco and Sonari.

Dogs and cats

The Trust’s work includes care for injured and stray dogs. For a cat in need, contact the team to confirm what help is available.

Birds and other animals

The Trust’s published journey mentions bird recovery and wildlife conservation. Ask the team about suitable support for the animal you have found.
