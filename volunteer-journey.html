VOLUNTEER JOURNEY

Many hands. A shared commitment.

  1. 2016

    A local beginning

    Suraj Kumar and friends began helping injured animals in Jamshedpur.

  2. 2017

    A growing circle

    Volunteers and supporters helped with care for cows and dogs, medicines and food.

  3. 2020

    Feeding through lockdown

    Volunteers continued street-animal feeding and field care during the COVID-19 lockdown.

  4. 2021

    Trust registration

    The organisation became a registered trust.

  5. 2024

    Community outreach

    The published journey records awareness activities in local schools.
